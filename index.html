Quick test:

Grapefruit & Citrus Juice

Inhibits the CYP3A4 enzyme in intestines, causing high systemic absorption of statins (Atorvastatin, Simvastatin) and calcium channel blockers (Amlodipine).

Risk: Statin toxicity, muscle breakdown (rhabdomyolysis)

Dairy & High Calcium (Milk, Yogurt)

Calcium chelates with antibiotics such as Doxycycline, Tetracycline, Ciprofloxacin, and Levofloxacin, blocking intestinal absorption.

Risk: Antibiotic treatment failure (Space by 2-4 hours)

Alcohol & Ethanol

Additive central nervous system depression with sedatives/opioids, severe liver toxicity with Paracetamol, and violent disulfiram-like vomiting with Metronidazole.

Risk: Severe CNS depression, hepatotoxicity, acute vomiting

High Potassium & Bananas / Salt Substitutes

ACE inhibitors (Lisinopril, Ramipril) and Potassium-sparing diuretics (Spironolactone) reduce potassium urinary excretion.

Risk: Fatal cardiac arrhythmias from hyperkalemia
